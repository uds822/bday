# ✨ Romantic Birthday Wish Experience ✨

A breathtaking, interactive, and emotional birthday experience designed with love. Complete with interactive 3D birthday cake, glowing candles you can blow out, realistic floating rose petals, fireworks and firecrackers celebration, a blooming flower bouquet, sweet love notes in floating balloons, romantic music box melody, and an unfolding wax-sealed love letter.

---

## 🌟 Interactive Highlights

1. **Surprise Entrance Curtain (`🎁 Gift Box`)**:
   - Tap the bouncing, glowing gift box to unveil the celebration.
   - Triggers the soft romantic music box melody, bursts of petals, and celebratory fireworks.

2. **Interactive 3D Birthday Cake (`🎂`)**:
   - 3-tier cake with whipped frosting drips and fresh strawberries.
   - Flickering candle flames.
   - **Tap the candles to blow them out**: Flames extinguish with realistic rising smoke, sound effects, a wish banner, and a grand celebratory firecracker show!

3. **Aerial Fireworks & Firecracker Show (`🎆 Crackers`)**:
   - Tap anywhere on the screen to launch custom rockets and cracker bursts.
   - Click **"Launch Firecrackers"** to fire a synchronized celebratory display featuring heart-shaped explosions, golden willows, and confetti streamers.

4. **Blooming Eternal Roses Bouquet (`💐 Flowers`)**:
   - Tap **"Bloom Flower Bouquet"** to watch stems grow and vibrant roses bloom in real-time with fluttering butterflies and a sweet romantic poem.

5. **Magical Floating Balloons (`🎈`)**:
   - Balloons drift softly up the screen.
   - Tap any balloon to pop it into confetti with sound effects and reveal a sweet compliment or love note!

6. **Wax-Sealed Love Letter & Reasons Why I Adore You (`💌`)**:
   - Heartfelt, customizable cursive letter signed with love.
   - Interactive love cards you can tap to feel cherished.

7. **100% Zero-Dependency Web Audio Synth (`🎵`)**:
   - No external audio files needed! All music box melodies and sound effects (firecrackers, blow whoosh, balloon pop, sparkle chimes) are synthesized directly in the browser.

## 🔗 How to Share the Link with Her

### Option 1: Instant Free Hosting via Netlify Drop (Takes 30 Seconds, No Code Needed!)
1. Open [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) in your browser.
2. Drag and drop the `bday` folder from your desktop directly onto that web page.
3. It will instantly give you a free public link (e.g., `https://romantic-bday-surprise.netlify.app`).
4. You can personalize the link by adding:
   `https://your-site.netlify.app/?to=HerName&from=Rajan`
5. Send the link to her on WhatsApp, Instagram, or iMessage!

### Option 2: GitHub Pages (Free Permanent Hosting)
1. Create a free repository on GitHub (e.g., `bday-surprise`).
2. Push or upload these files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`, `audio.js`, `petals.js`, `fireworks.js`, `flowers.js`).
3. Go to **Settings** -> **Pages** -> Select **Deploy from Branch (main)** -> Save.
4. Your link will be live at:
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/bday-surprise/?to=HerName&from=Rajan`

### Option 3: Using the In-App "Personalize & Share" Button
1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Click **"🔗 Share Link"** or **"✍️ Personalize & Share"**.
3. Type her name, your name, and any custom love note.
4. Click **"Copy Personalized Link"** to automatically generate and copy the customized link to your clipboard!

## 🎨 URL Personalization Parameters

You can customize the whole experience directly through the URL query string:
- `?to=HerName` (e.g. `?to=Aanya` or `?to=My%20Angel`) -> Sets her name everywhere on the site!
- `?from=YourName` (e.g. `?from=Rajan`) -> Sets who it's from on the love letter!
- `?msg=CustomNote` -> Customizes the opening message of the letter!
